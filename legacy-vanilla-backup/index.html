Waste2Worth (W2W)

// Gamified AI Waste Ecosystem • Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash & Supabase

Launch Live App ↗

Waste2Worth (W2W) transforms everyday waste management into a gamified, reward-driven experience. By combining real-time artificial intelligence vision with Supabase backend services, W2W enables users to scan waste items, learn proper disposal steps, discover creative DIY upcycling ideas, and track verified carbon savings.

🤖 1. AI Vision Waste Scanner
Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash. Instant camera or upload analysis categorizing items into Recyclable, Compostable, Hazardous, Landfill, and Upcyclable with real-time CO₂ & Water saved metrics and step-by-step guidance.
Gemini 2.5 Flash Multimodal AI CO₂ Metrics
💳 2. Carbon Wallet & Rewards
Automated eco credits engine rewarding verified scans. Features up to 3x daily streak multiplier (7-day streak), badge milestones, and time-gated anti-abuse yield protection against spam scans.
Streak Multiplier Anti-Abuse Logic Gamified Wallet
🛍️ 3. Upcycling Marketplace
Peer-to-peer trading to buy, sell, or donate waste materials and upcycled items. Supabase storage uploads (listing-images bucket), direct WhatsApp & Email chat buttons, and category & city filtering.
P2P Trading Supabase Storage Direct Chat
🏫 4. Campus & Org Dashboard
Role-based accounts for Students, Individuals, and Companies. Campus invite codes (e.g. GREEN2026), real-time collective team carbon leaderboards, and admin moderation.
Role-Based Auth Invite Codes Live Leaderboard
📍 5. Eco Facilities Map & Directory
Interactive geolocation discovery of nearby recycling hubs, e-waste drop-offs, composting centers, and upcycling workshops with instant search and status filtering.
Geo Directory Drop-off Locator Interactive Map
👥 6. Social Network & Leaderboards
Unique 8-character friend codes (e.g. A7B9C1D2). Send and accept friend requests to compare weekly carbon scores, achievements, and eco streaks.
Friend Codes Weekly Competitions Social Graph
